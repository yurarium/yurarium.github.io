なぜこの画面があるか / why this page exists

Listing sites report that a work updated. Those reports are inputs. Earlier versions put unconfirmed ones into the update feed marked 要確認, which asked readers to hold a question only this system can answer. Every claim now ends in one disposition, and only the open ones represent work outstanding.

A refutation is only recorded where our own capture is deep enough to support it — at least three chapters of the platform's own history, and no long gap between the claim and the newest chapter we hold. Those two guards exist because “the platform lists nothing” and “our fetch came back thin” look identical from inside a build. Where a guard fails the claim stays open rather than being written off, and the source freshness table above is what makes the difference checkable.
