Dates are the earlier of the platform's claim and when this database first saw the release, fixed at that point and never revised. These feeds carry only Atom updated — last-modified — and platforms rewrite it in bulk during refreshes and imports, so a later change is recorded but not followed. 初確認 marks a date inherited rather than observed; 日付要確認 marks a run whose timestamps look rewritten.
