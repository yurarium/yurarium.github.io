Updates to yuri web manga, newest first.

Two kinds of entry. Most come from a publisher's own feed or work pages and are confirmed. Those marked 要確認 are reported by Web漫画アンテナ or 百合ナビ and not confirmed against the platform — taken as provisionally true that something updated, on the date claimed. They carry no classification and establish nothing beyond that.

Two models of "update". All counts every update including paid-only chapters. Free counts only what can be read without paying — including rate-limited free (one chapter a day with an account) and chapters that have moved out from behind a paywall, which is an update if free reading is what you are watching for.

新連載 a first numbered chapter or a one-shot · 新話 a later numbered chapter · その他 notice, trial or reprint · — no chapter information. These are inferred from what we hold, not stated by the publisher; 新連載 means first seen here.
